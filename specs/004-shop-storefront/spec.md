# Shop storefront

Customer-facing pages for the published catalog and signed-in checkout. Admin screens are out of scope.

## Pages

- The storefront is Persian and light for now. Home is a full-viewport image with the headline on the image. The header has the OY mark, a products link, and a menu for cart and account. It does not list garment types.
- Products lists published t-shirts and hoodies together. The shopper can filter by garment type and sort by newest, lowest price, or highest price.
- A product page shows the design, color swatches, sizes, stock, price in toman, and add to cart. Photos for the selected color swipe horizontally, and a photo opens full screen. A missing color and size cannot be selected.
- Cart shows lines for the signed-in customer. A guest is asked to sign in. Checkout, addresses, and orders require an account.
- Checkout chooses one saved Iranian address and one flat shipping method, then places an online-payment order. The confirmation shows the order number and the pending-payment window.

## Out of scope

- Admin catalog and fulfillment screens.
- A payment gateway. The order stays pending payment after it is placed.
